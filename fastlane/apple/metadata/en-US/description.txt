AACFree is a free AAC (augmentative and alternative communication) app for autistic and non-verbal people of every age, and for the families, teachers and therapists who support them. Tap picture-and-word buttons to build a sentence, and AACFree speaks it aloud.

It is built around what AAC users, parents and speech-language pathologists ask for: words that never move, "no" and "stop" always within reach, fast typing, and privacy by default.

FREE, PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
• Free forever. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.
• No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking.
• Everything stays on your iPhone or iPad and works without the internet.
• Open source.

FOR THE PERSON COMMUNICATING
• Core words that stay put. Choose a home layout from 12 big buttons to 48. Buttons never move, so words can be learned by motor memory. Hidden words keep their place.
• 25 topic folders with about 770 words and 740 built-in pictures: food, drinks, feelings, people, places, play, school, body, health and more.
• Colour-coded word types (Modified Fitzgerald Key).
• Yes, no, stop, help, wait and break are on every screen. Talking is never locked.
• Word forms when you want them: go, went, going; plurals; bigger, biggest.
• A keyboard with private word prediction.
• Ready-made phrases for needs, feelings, boundaries, chat, questions and emergencies. Save your own phrases and scripts, which helps gestalt language processors.
• Tools: visual schedule, first/then board, visual timer, choice board, feelings and pain scales, a "where does it hurt" body map, an About me card and a drawing pad.
• Use any voice on your device, including enhanced voices, and set its speed and pitch. Record a family member's voice on any button.
• English, Spanish, French and Portuguese, with bilingual labels.
• Access: hold-to-select, ignore repeated taps, switch scanning, head or eye pointing with the front camera (processed on the device), large text, dark and high-contrast themes.

FOR PARENTS, TEACHERS AND THERAPISTS
• Settings sit behind a 2-second hold, a PIN or a math question, so a curious tap never changes a board.
• Edit in place: change any button's word, picture, colour or voice. Use the built-in symbols, emoji or your own photos. Undo any change.
• Bring a board from another AAC app: take a screenshot or photo of it and AACFree rebuilds it with every word in the same place, reading the words on the device. Or import Open Board files (.obz).
• Find word shows step by step where any word lives, which makes modelling easier.
• Never lose work: every change saves straight away, with daily snapshots, automatic backups to the Files app or iCloud Drive, and backup files you can lock with a passcode.
• Sync between devices change by change through one shared file in iCloud Drive or another cloud drive, encrypted with your family's passcode. No account.
• Print paper boards as a low-tech backup for the pool, bath time or a flat battery.
• Optional private usage insights, off unless you turn them on, never leaving the device.
• Several profiles on one device. Works well with Guided Access to stay in the app.
