Privacy policy

Caleb AAC (web app and Android app org.calebaac.app). Effective 12 November 2026.

Caleb does not collect or share any personal data.

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no tracking and no ads.
  • Everything you create in Caleb is stored only on your device.
  • We have no servers, so there is nowhere for your data to go.

What stays on your device

Boards, words, phrases, photos, voice recordings, settings, profiles and the optional usage log are saved in the app's own storage on your device (IndexedDB). They are never uploaded by Caleb. The developers cannot see them.

Usage insights and learned word prediction are off unless you turn them on. When they are on, they still stay on the device. A CSV export is created only when you ask for one.

Camera

Caleb uses the camera only when you choose to:

  • take a photo for a button or symbol. The photo is saved on the device with your boards.
  • use the optional head or eye pointer (an access feature). The camera image is processed on the device, live, to move the pointer. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Caleb never uses the camera in the background, and you can refuse or remove the permission at any time in Android settings.

Microphone

Caleb uses the microphone only when you press record to make a voice clip for a button or phrase. The recording is saved on the device. Caleb never listens in the background and never sends recordings anywhere.

Internet

Caleb works fully offline. It uses the internet only for:

  • Online symbol search, when you search for more pictures. Your search words are sent to ARASAAC (api.arasaac.org) and the pictures are downloaded from static.arasaac.org. Like any website, ARASAAC's servers can see your device's IP address. See arasaac.org for their policies.
  • Text-to-speech voices. Caleb speaks with the voices of your device's text-to-speech engine. If you choose an online voice, the engine (for example, Speech Services by Google) may send the text being spoken to its provider. That is controlled by the engine you have installed and its own privacy policy. Offline voices keep speech on the device.

Caleb sends nothing else over the internet.

Backups, sync and sharing

  • Backup files, Open Board Format (.obz) exports, printed boards and CSV exports are created only when you choose, and you decide where they go.
  • Sync files for moving boards between devices are created only when you choose, and are encrypted with a passcode you set.
  • When you share a message or a file, Android's share sheet hands it to the app you pick. That app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Android Auto Backup: Android may include Caleb's data in your own Google account backup, as it does for most apps. That backup belongs to you and is managed by Google, not by Caleb. You can turn it off in Android Settings → Google → Backup.

Children

Caleb is made for people of all ages, including children. Because Caleb collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Caleb is open source, so you can check exactly what it does in the source code.

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/edrock200/caleb/issues.
