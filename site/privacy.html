Privacy policy

AACFree (web app and Android app org.calebaac.app). Effective 12 November 2026.

AACFree does not collect or share any personal data.

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no tracking and no ads.
  • Everything you create in AACFree is stored only on your device.
  • We have no servers, so there is nowhere for your data to go.
  • Backups and sync go only to a place you choose, and can be locked with your own passcode.

What stays on your device

Boards, words, phrases, photos, voice recordings, settings, profiles and the optional usage log are saved in the app's own storage on your device (IndexedDB). They are never uploaded by AACFree. The developers cannot see them.

Usage insights and learned word prediction are off unless you turn them on. When they are on, they still stay on the device. A CSV export is created only when you ask for one.

Camera

AACFree uses the camera only when you choose to:

  • take a photo for a button or symbol. The photo is saved on the device with your boards.
  • import a board from a picture. The photo or screenshot is read on the device: the words are recognised by text recognition built into AACFree, without the internet. The picture is never uploaded and is not kept, except the parts you choose to use as button pictures.
  • use the optional head or eye pointer (an access feature). The camera image is processed on the device, live, to move the pointer. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

AACFree never uses the camera in the background, and you can refuse or remove the permission at any time in Android settings.

Microphone

AACFree uses the microphone only when you press record to make a voice clip for a button or phrase. The recording is saved on the device. AACFree never listens in the background and never sends recordings anywhere.

Internet

AACFree works fully offline. It uses the internet only for:

  • Online symbol search, when you search for more pictures. Your search words are sent to ARASAAC (api.arasaac.org) and the pictures are downloaded from static.arasaac.org. Like any website, ARASAAC's servers can see your device's IP address. See arasaac.org for their policies.
  • Missing pictures. Every built-in picture comes with the app. If a board uses an ARASAAC picture that isn't included (for example, boards imported from another AAC app), AACFree downloads that one picture from static.arasaac.org when it is shown. Only the picture's number is requested, nothing about the person. You can turn this off in Settings → Lock → “Download missing pictures from ARASAAC”; the picture is then left blank.
  • Backups and sync to a cloud file you pick (see below). AACFree writes only to the file you choose, through your device's own file picker.
  • Text-to-speech voices. AACFree speaks with the voices of your device's text-to-speech engine. If you choose an online voice, the engine (for example, Speech Services by Google) may send the text being spoken to its provider. That is controlled by the engine you have installed and its own privacy policy. Offline voices keep speech on the device.

AACFree sends nothing else over the internet.

Backups, sync and sharing

  • Backup files, Open Board Format (.obz) exports, printed boards and CSV exports are created only when you choose, and you decide where they go.
  • Automatic backups, if you turn them on, are saved to a folder on the device or to one file you pick (for example in Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox). You can lock them with a passcode, and you choose whether photos, recordings and message history are included.
  • Sync files for moving boards between devices are created only when you choose, and are encrypted with a passcode you set.
  • Automatic sync, if you turn it on, keeps devices in step through one file your family picks in a cloud drive. The data is always encrypted on the device with your family's passcode before it is written, and it goes only to that file. AACFree has no server and no account, so neither the developers nor anyone without the passcode can read it. The cloud provider's own policy applies to storing the encrypted file.
  • The settings PIN stays on the device. It is not put in unencrypted backups.
  • When you share a message or a file, Android's share sheet hands it to the app you pick. That app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Android Auto Backup: Android may include AACFree's data in your own Google account backup, as it does for most apps. That backup belongs to you and is managed by Google, not by AACFree. You can turn it off in Android Settings → Google → Backup.

Children

AACFree is made for people of all ages, including children. Because AACFree collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. AACFree is open source, so you can check exactly what it does in the source code.

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/edrock200/caleb/issues.
