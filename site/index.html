Caleb is an AAC communication app for autistic people and the people who love them. It works on any browser and on Android, fully offline.

Built from what AAC users and families asked for

We read what autistic AAC users, parents and speech therapists say about existing apps. Caleb is designed around their top requests and their biggest frustrations.

✋

Words stay put

Core words sit in fixed places, so they can be learned by muscle memory. Grow vocabulary by revealing hidden words. Nothing gets rearranged.

🗣️

Talk fast

Symbols, a keyboard with word prediction, saved phrases and recent sentences all live in one app. There is no switching apps mid-conversation.

🛑

“No” is always there

Yes, no, stop, help, wait and break stay on every screen. Talking is never locked.

🔤

Grammar on demand

Long-press a word for go → went / going / goes, plurals and more. Nothing changes automatically.

💬

Scripts & gestalts

Save whole phrases, movie lines and scripts with one tap. This supports gestalt language processors.

♿

Access for every body

Hold-to-select, ignore repeated taps, 1- and 2-switch scanning with auditory cues, high contrast, a dark theme and adjustable text size.

🎙️

Real voices

Choose any voice on the device and set its speed and pitch. Record a family member's voice for special buttons.

🗓️

Daily supports

Visual schedules, first/then boards, a visual timer, choice boards, feelings and pain scales, a body map, an “About me” card and a drawing pad.

🌙

Not childish

A calm “Clean” style for teens and adults, real-world phrases, and the person's own photos.

For parents, teachers and therapists

  • Edit in place: tap any button to change its word, picture, colour or voice. Tap two squares to swap them. Undo any change.
  • Symbol library in category folders: create your own categories and symbols, and build a board folder from any category in one tap.
  • Pictures for everything: 740 ARASAAC symbols built in, thousands more searchable online, plus camera photos and emoji.
  • Grown-up lock: a 2-second hold, a PIN or a math question keeps settings safe from accidental taps.
  • Never lose work: automatic daily snapshots, one-file backups and moving to a new device.
  • Paper boards: print boards as a low-tech backup for the pool, bath time or a flat battery.
  • Find word: shows step by step where any word lives. Great for modelling.
  • Private insights: off unless you turn it on. Shows most-used and new words, with CSV export for your SLP. Nothing ever leaves the device.
  • Share boards with other AAC apps via the Open Board Format (.obz).
  • Profiles for siblings or classrooms on one device.
“AAC access needs to be 24/7.” Autistic AAC users

Caleb follows AAC best practice: model language by using the device yourself, presume competence, never take the device away, and treat babbling and exploring on the device as learning. The in-app Help & tips explain how.

Web & iPad / iPhone

  1. Open the Caleb web app in Chrome, Edge or Safari.
  2. Install it to your home screen: Install app in Chrome, or Share → Add to Home Screen on iPad/iPhone.
  3. It then works offline like a normal app.

Android

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone or tablet (Android 7+).
  2. Open it and allow “Install unknown apps” when asked.
  3. Tip: turn on App pinning in Android Settings so Caleb can't be closed by accident.
